# Code of Conduct

VZT Video-Intel follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Short version

- Treat people with respect, including when they disagree with you
- Assume good intent, ask before assuming bad
- No harassment, doxxing, or personal attacks
- No spam, no NSFW content in repos or issues
- Disagree on technical points by referencing data, not identity

## Enforcement

Report issues to **conduct@vzt.dev**. Maintainers will review every report within 7 days. We may warn, mute, or ban accounts at our discretion.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, PRs, discussions, Discord, in-person events. Conduct outside project spaces that materially affects the community is also in scope.
